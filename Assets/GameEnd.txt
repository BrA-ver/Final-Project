The player will be able to open a list of all the suspects and explain why that person is innocent or guilty.

In order to determine the guilty suspect the player needs to prove 3 things. Motive, intent and opportunity. 
- Motive - why did they want to kill the musician?
- Intent - what did they use to kill the musician and did they intend to kill them?
- Opportunity - when did they decide to strike. 

The player will need to prove these using the evidence they collected.

E.g. Suspect 1: Janitor 

- Motive - the musician was always rude to them because they were in a position of power.
- Intent - they had access to the poison that killed the victim.
- Opportunity - they were outside smoking at the time of the murder , therefore they are innocent. 

Only when the player has all three can they conclude that the suspect is guilty. 

E.g. 2 Suspect 2: Bartender 

- Motive - The musician broke their arm in a car accident and refuses to pay their medical bill, leaving them in debt. 
- Intent - They borrowed the janitor's key ring while the janitor was on break, giving them access to the poison.
- Opportunity - the security guard always leaves the building when the musician performs their final, which the allowed the bartender 
		to enter the musician's dressing room undetected to spike their drink, therefore they are guilty. 

Only when the player has all three can they conclude that the suspect is guilty.
